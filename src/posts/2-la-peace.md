I've started playing badminton over the past month. I've been playing twice a week, consistently. Quite an interesting sport. Not sure why I never played this earlier. Hitting the shuttle is very satisfying.  
I don't usually train cardio at all. I thought it would be hard for me to keep up with the stamina demands, given the fact that it's the fastest sport out there (I'm gonna challenge this claim in the near future). That, however, did not seem to be true. My heart rate was always well under limits, fatigue rate was low as well. My fitness band reported every badminton session as a "warm up".  
I never really scurried around the court in panic as well. That might be the determinant for the lack of fatigue after every session. Super fun and super satisfying though. I can play for hours on end.
